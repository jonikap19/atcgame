        GLIDEPATH

        Bring arrivals down onto the glide path, launch departures out through their exit gates, and keep everyone 1000 ft or 3 miles apart.

        HOW TO PLAY
        • Sand planes are arrivals. Drag from one to draw its path. End the path inside a dashed landing cone to clear it to land.
        • Arrivals must be low enough to land. Each label shows altitude↓target speed, with altitude in hundreds of feet. Get them down to about 030 before the cone.
        • Draw a circle at the end of a path and the plane will hold there, going round the way you drew it.
        • Tap a plane to change its altitude and speed. You can also use the arrow keys or the mouse wheel.
        • Blue planes are departures. Launch them from the Departures panel, then route them out through their lettered exit gate, at 050 or higher.
        • The wind sometimes shifts. You get a warning first, then landings and takeoffs swap to the other end of the runways.
        • Save routes in Plan mode. Any plane can join a route: drag the plane onto the route's start ring.
        • A dashed amber ring means two planes will conflict soon. A red ring means they have, and you're losing points. If they touch, the shift is over.
